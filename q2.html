Bad Website Design Review

For this review, I chose Arngren.net, a website that displays many types of products, including electronics, toys, and vehicles. The website is an example of how a page can become difficult to use when too many items compete for attention.

Visit Arngren.net

10 Reasons the Website Design Is Poor

  1. Cluttered layout: Many product images and descriptions are packed closely together, making the page feel crowded.
  2. Weak visual hierarchy: It is difficult to tell which products or sections should receive attention first.
  3. Inconsistent spacing: Items do not have enough consistent spacing to separate one product from another.
  4. Small text: Some product descriptions and labels are difficult to scan quickly.
  5. Too many images: The large number of product pictures makes it harder to focus on one item at a time.
  6. Busy presentation: Text, prices, links, and images compete for the visitor's attention.
  7. Inconsistent alignment: Product information does not always follow a clear, consistent grid.
  8. Hard-to-scan categories: The many product categories and links can take time to look through.
  9. Unclear priorities: The page does not make one main action or message stand out clearly.
  10. Limited visual consistency: The presentation feels less organized than a catalog with consistent product cards and spacing.

Conclusion

In my opinion, the website could be improved by organizing products into clear categories, using consistent product cards, improving spacing, and making text easier to read. These changes would help visitors find products more easily.
